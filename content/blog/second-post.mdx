The parts of an interface people notice least are usually the ones that took
the most care: timing on a transition, the weight of a border, how text wraps
at the edge of a container.

## Why it matters

None of these things are the feature. Nobody asks for "slightly better
easing" — but they can tell when it's missing. Consistency across a whole
site is what makes it feel considered rather than assembled.

If you want a longer take on this idea, [Frank Chimero's essay on craft](https://frankchimero.com/writing/the-inferno-of-independence/)
is worth a read.

That attention is the whole point of keeping this site's design system small
and consistent instead of piling on one-off styles per page.
